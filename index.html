        About this atlas

        Every bone here is generated procedurally in code — there are no downloaded mesh files. Cranial vault bones are carved along the real coronal, sagittal, lambdoid and squamous sutures; long bones are swept along curved axes with rotating elliptical cross-sections; the vertebral column follows a measured sagittal curve carrying all four normal spinal curvatures.

        Male vs female

        Switching profiles rebuilds the entire skeleton using the differences forensic anthropologists rely on to determine sex — above all the pelvis.

        Sources

        Anatomical descriptions, landmarks, articulations, ossification timetables and clinical notes are cross-checked against Gray's Anatomy (42nd ed.), Moore's Clinically Oriented Anatomy (8th ed.), Terminologia Anatomica (2nd ed.) and Netter's Atlas of Human Anatomy (7th ed.). Gait kinematics follow Winter, Biomechanics and Motor Control of Human Movement.

        This is a study aid. It is a stylised model, not a patient-specific or diagnostic image, and must not be used for clinical decision-making.
